Hardware Requirements

Planned parts: ESP32, MPU6050/GY-521, SW-420 vibration sensor, GPS NEO-6M, compatible GSM module, LED, buzzer, cancel button, suitable power supplies and wiring.

Confirm the exact specifications of your components before connecting power. Do not power a GSM module directly from the ESP32 3.3 V pin unless its documentation explicitly permits it.
